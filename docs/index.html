03 / FOLLOW ONE PLAYER'S CREDIT

Weighted differences, not a label.

For each predecessor coalition S, compare v(S plus player) with v(S). Its weight is 1 / [d * choose(d-1, |S|)], where d is the number of atomic players. Equal-size coalitions get equal weights; different sizes need not.

Groups switch all members together. The three-way product assigns the atomic group ab a share of 1/2; summing individual a and b gives 2/3. They are different games. No arbitrary allocation back to members, and no Owen within-group value.
